See your card base and enriched volume at a glance.

The Overview is the first screen in LuneHub. It shows how many cardholders transact and how many transactions Lune enriched.

  • Cardholders with transactions for the period you select.
  • Enriched transactions against the volume in your plan.
  • Transactions by date day by day, for the last month.

One view of customers, transactions and spend.

Select a date range, a currency and a country. The dashboard shows the totals and the split.

  • Total customers and total transactions.
  • Total spend and spend per customer.
  • Spend by category and brand in one chart.

Find the merchants where your customers spend most.

The Brand explorer lists each enriched merchant with six columns. Your team filters and sorts the list to find the brands that matter.

  • Brand and category for each enriched merchant.
  • Total spend and transactions for the period you select.
  • Customers who paid that brand with your cards.
  • Spend per customer to compare brands of different size.
Filter

Find a brand in seconds

Search by brand name. Filter by category. Set a range for spend, transactions, customers or spend per customer.

Sort

Rank brands your way

Sort the list by spend, transactions, customers or spend per customer.

Scope

Select the period and the market

Select a date range, a currency and a country. The list shows only that scope.

Which brands do most of our customers use?Sort by customers.

See spend for each category.

The Category explorer lists all 19 Lune categories with their totals. Your team sees where card spend goes and how many customers are behind it.

  • Total spend and transactions for each category.
  • Customers who spend in each category.
  • The number of subcategories below each category.
